Projects & Welfare

Where every Tour ticket goes.

The League services special needs children and adults along the Mississippi Gulf Coast — and provides for other welfare needs throughout the year. Below are the projects we support, plus one-time gifts whenever we can.

🎒

Backpack Buddies

Provides elementary school children who qualify for free or reduced-price meals a sack of healthy, easy-to-prepare foods for the weekend.

⚾

Buddy Ball

Sports and leisure activities for individuals with special needs — built around belonging, friendship, and play.

🚶

Buddy Walk

Established in 1995 by the National Down Syndrome Society, the Buddy Walk promotes acceptance and inclusion of people with Down Syndrome.

🏕

Camp Able

A unique camping experience for people with diverse abilities — outdoors, together, and on their own terms.

⚖

CASA of Harrison County

Court Appointed Special Advocates — trained volunteers appointed by a judicial officer to advocate for a child who is under the jurisdiction of the courts due to abuse or neglect.

♿

Disability Connection

Empowering individuals with disabilities to participate fully in all aspects of community life.

🕊

Gulf Coast Center for Non-Violence

Support and services for victims of domestic violence and sexual assault — and for the children of those victims.

🍽

Holiday Baskets for Families

Holiday food baskets delivered to families in need across the Gulf Coast every season.

🔔

Salvation Army

Helping families and individuals during natural disasters, holidays, addictions, and human trafficking recovery — and offering community care. Members also ring the bell each November.

🌷

Women's Resource Center

24-hour, 7-day-a-week crisis intervention services for women and children who are victims of domestic violence.

One-time gifts

And whenever there is a need...

In addition to the projects we support every year, the League makes several one-time donations to people and causes that the moment calls for — a family in crisis, a student in need, a partner organization with an urgent ask.

To provide these services, an annual Christmas Tour of Homes is held each December. All proceeds go toward our projects.
