Scholarship Program

Investing in students who lift others up.

The Civic League Scholarship is awarded to students with special needs, and to students pursuing careers that serve people with special needs.

Who we support

Two kinds of students sit at the heart of our scholarship:

  • Students with special needs who are continuing their education and pursuing a path that's right for them.
  • Students entering careers of service — education, therapy, social work, healthcare, advocacy — who plan to work with people with special needs.

Awards are funded entirely by the proceeds of the Christmas Tour of Homes and the generosity of our community partners.

How to apply

Three simple steps

✦
1

Request the application

Email our Scholarship Chair at civicleagueofgulfport@gmail.com with the subject "Scholarship application request" and your name, school, and intended field of study.

2

Submit your materials

Complete the application along with a short personal statement, an unofficial transcript, and a letter of recommendation from a teacher, counselor, or mentor.

3

Hear back from the committee

Our Scholarship Committee reviews applications each fall and spring, then presents recommendations to the Board. We'll be in touch either way.

Frequently asked

Good things to know

✦

Who can apply?

Students with special needs continuing their education, and students from the Mississippi Gulf Coast preparing for a career serving people with special needs.

When are deadlines?

Applications are reviewed twice a year, with the fall deadline announced at our October meeting and the spring deadline in early spring.

Are there multiple awards?

Yes — the League awards multiple scholarships annually, and the number is set each year based on the success of the Christmas Tour of Homes.

Can I be nominated?

Absolutely. Teachers, counselors, and family members may write to the Scholarship Chair to share a student we should reach out to.
