About the Tour

The first Christmas Tour of Homes was held in 1959, visiting the homes of Mr. and Mrs. Henry Haller, Mrs. M. M. Snelling, and Mr. and Mrs. A. J. Schultz. Through the years the homes on tour have ranged from Biloxi to Pass Christian, with most in Gulfport itself.

The Tour has been held every December since 1959 — pausing only three times in its history: 1969 for Hurricane Camille, 2005 for Hurricane Katrina, and 2020 for the global pandemic.

It is the League's only fundraiser. Every dollar from ticket sales supports our projects and scholarships.

A little history

Sixty-six tours and counting

✦

Through hurricanes and pandemics, this tradition has carried on because Gulf Coast neighbors keep opening their doors.

  1. 1959

    The first Tour visits the Haller, Snelling, and Schultz homes.

  2. 1969

    Hurricane Camille — the only cancellation in the Tour's first decade.

  3. 2005

    Hurricane Katrina — the Tour pauses; the League refocuses on relief work.

  4. 2020

    The global pandemic — the Tour pauses for the third and only other time in its history.

  5. 2026

    Sunday, December 6 — the next chapter, with you in it.
