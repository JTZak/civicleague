Since 1947

Our History

From eighteen women at a polio clinic to nearly eighty years of service on the Mississippi Gulf Coast.

In 1947, a group of eighteen civic-minded women formed a service group with a focus on staffing a polio clinic in the city of Gulfport. Their goals were to check in patients (adults and children alike), assist the staff, and provide activities to entertain children while they waited. They staffed the clinic daily.

Today, the Gulfport Civic League has evolved into a thirty-five-member community-minded group of women dedicated to assisting those individuals on the Gulf Coast who have special needs. The League also takes on many other community projects, meeting the needs of as many as possible with the money collected from its only fundraiser, the Christmas Tour of Homes.

In the League's first yearbook, typewritten with crayon-colored images, the motto was "We Pass for What We Are." The flower was the Gold Chrysanthemum. The color was green. Both remain the League's emblems to this day.

The Christmas Tour of Homes

The first Christmas Tour of Homes was held in 1959, visiting the homes of Mr. and Mrs. Henry Haller, Mrs. M. M. Snelling, and Mr. and Mrs. A. J. Schultz. Through the years, the homes on tour have not always been within the city limits of Gulfport; homes from Biloxi to Pass Christian have also been included.

The tour has been held every year since 1959, pausing only three times: in 1969 for Hurricane Camille, in 2005 for Hurricane Katrina, and in 2020 for the Global Pandemic. The League is forever thankful to the homeowners who so graciously open their homes each December.

  • 1947

    Eighteen charter members form the Civic League to staff Gulfport's polio clinic.

  • 1959

    The first Christmas Tour of Homes is held, beginning the League's signature tradition.

  • 1969

    The tour pauses for Hurricane Camille, the first missed year in a decade of tours.

  • 2005

    Hurricane Katrina devastates the Coast; the League pauses the tour and turns to recovery.

  • 2020

    The global pandemic pauses the tour for only the third time in its history.

  • Today

    Thirty-five members strong, funding projects and scholarships across the Gulf Coast.
