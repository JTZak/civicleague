About the Tour

Sixty-seven years of open doors

The first Christmas Tour of Homes was held in 1959, visiting the homes of Mr. and Mrs. Henry Haller, Mrs. M. M. Snelling, and Mr. and Mrs. A. J. Schultz. Ever since, gracious homeowners from Biloxi to Pass Christian have opened their doors each December.

The tour is the League's only fundraiser. Every ticket funds Backpack Buddies, Buddy Ball, Camp Able, scholarships, holiday baskets, and crisis support services across the Coast.

  • Held every year since 1959, pausing only for Camille, Katrina, and the 2020 Global Pandemic
  • A Silent Auction accompanies the tour each year
  • Homes announced in the fall; follow along on Facebook
We are always so thankful to the homeowners who so graciously open their homes for our tour.
