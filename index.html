Who We Are

Congenial women, common purpose

Founded in 1947 by eighteen civic-minded women staffing Gulfport's polio clinic, the Civic League today is a thirty-five-member organization serving those with special needs across the Coast.

Our Work

Where your ticket dollars go

Every dollar raised by the Christmas Tour of Homes, our only fundraiser, goes directly to community projects and scholarships.

Backpack Buddies

Weekend sacks of healthy, easy-to-prepare food for elementary school children who qualify for free or reduced-price meals.

Buddy Ball & Buddy Walk

Sports, leisure activities, and inclusion programs for individuals with special needs, including the Gulf Coast Buddy Walk.

Scholarships

Awards for students with special needs and for students pursuing careers that serve people with special needs.

Shelter & Crisis Support

Support for the Women's Resource Center, Gulf Coast Center for Non-Violence, and CASA of Harrison County.

Holiday Baskets

Holiday food baskets for local families in need, plus Salvation Army bell ringing and disaster relief support each season.

Camp Able & Disability Connections

Unique camping experiences for people of diverse abilities and empowerment for full participation in community life.

A Cherished Tradition

The Christmas Tour of Homes

Since 1959, the people of the Gulf Coast have opened beautifully decorated homes, from Biloxi to Pass Christian, to benefit our neighbors with special needs. It is the League's only fundraiser, and every ticket makes a difference.

  • Sunday, December 6, 2026: mark your calendar
  • Tickets are $20 and benefit League projects and scholarships
  • Held every year since 1959, pausing only for Camille, Katrina, and 2020
